# l6-ekf-pf — EKF & Particle Filters

Concept: Estimation when the world stops being linear: EKF = Kalman filter on a first-order Taylor expansion (Jacobians of motion/measurement models, evaluated at the current estimate) and where that breaks; particle filter = sample-weight-resample belief representation with degeneracy/effective-sample-size N_eff; choosing KF vs EKF vs PF for a given problem.
Learner prerequisites: l6-kalman Gold (the linear loop must be automatic), l5-lie-se3 (poses/bearings — why robot states live on manifolds and angles wrap). Jacobians from l2-derivatives return here with real payload.
What beginners commonly misunderstand: (1) What exactly gets linearized — learners say "the system" instead of "the models, at the current estimate, fresh every step"; they then miss that a bad estimate poisons its own Jacobian (the EKF's core failure loop). (2) Angle wrap-around in innovations (z − h(x) crossing ±π) — the #1 practical EKF bug in range-bearing problems. (3) Believing the EKF propagates the true covariance rather than the linearization's covariance — divergence with a confident P follows. (4) Resampling as harmless cleanup: it trades weight variance for particle depletion; resampling every step kills diversity (N_eff exists to time it). (5) Reading "PF handles anything" and missing the particle-count curse in higher dimensions.
